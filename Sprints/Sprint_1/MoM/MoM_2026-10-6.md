## 2. Key Discussion Points

- If we request data that is not public, we will need to complete data management
training through the CITI Program (Social Behavioral Conduct course and
Research Security Training course).

- Reviewed thematic analysis examples, including the Comparative Manifestos
Project, which coded political party platforms into themes by sentence and
paragraph. Its codebook (manifesto-project.wzb.eu) can be used for examples.

- Reviewed the list of desired features: citations and themes that stay highlighted
in documents (possibly toggleable, with user-chosen colors per code),
querying whether themes are present, searching for specific words and like-
terms, and distinguishing theme search from synonym search.

- Discussed whether to train a model to recognize and code a specific topic or to
use an existing language encoding model.

## 3. Decisions Made

- We decided we will meet as a group to decide on an approach: an application
that is good at qualitative data analysis for a specific topic, or one that is decent
at it for any topic.
- We decided we will use the data documents in the Teams channel to test our
application.
